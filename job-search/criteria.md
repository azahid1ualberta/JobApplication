# Job Search Criteria — Abdullah Al Zahid

Edit this file any time; the daily automation reads it fresh every morning.

## Target field
Transportation planning / transportation engineering (NOT software development).
This matches Abdullah's actual resume: transportation planner background, GIS,
traffic/rail simulation, spatial data analysis.

## Seniority — READ THIS FIRST
Abdullah held the title **Transportation Planner** at Metrolinx. He does not want
to move down from that level. Planner-grade is the FLOOR, not the target.

Target band (search these actively):
- Planner / Transportation Planner (full grade, not Assistant or Junior)
- Senior Transportation Planner / Senior Planner
- Senior Analyst / Senior Advisor / Advisor
- Specialist / Senior Specialist
- Project Manager, Team Lead, Supervisor, Program Manager, Manager
  (where the work is transportation planning, transit or mobility)
- Consultant / Senior Consultant (transportation practice)

DO NOT include (these are downgrades — skip them even if the work looks relevant):
- Assistant / Associate / Junior / Entry-level / Graduate anything
- Intern, Co-op, Student, Trainee
- Engineer in Training (EIT), or any title containing "in Training"
- Technician, Technologist, Technical Assistant
- Grade-numbered entry roles (e.g. "Analyst 1", "Planner I")
- Coordinator, where the posting reads as administrative support rather than
  professional planning work

Experience thresholds: Abdullah has ~4.5 years of relevant experience plus an
M.Sc. in Transportation Engineering. Postings asking 5-8 years are IN SCOPE —
apply anyway and flag the gap in the tracker. Skip roles explicitly requiring
15+ years, or a licensed P.Eng where the posting does not say "or equivalent
experience."

## Judge the WORK, not the title string
Employers use different vocabularies for the same grade. Metrolinx, for example,
uses "Advisor" and "Specialist" where other employers use "Planner" or "Analyst"
— "Senior Advisor, Rapid Transit Analysis" is exactly the kind of role Abdullah
wants. Never reject a posting only because its title is not on the keyword list.
Read the responsibilities and the years-of-experience ask, then decide.

Equally, never accept a posting only because its title contains "Senior" — check
that the scope and pay actually sit at or above Planner grade.

## Salary
No hard minimum set by Abdullah. Reference point for judging a downgrade: his
current municipal planning band is roughly $47-$51/hour (about $92,000-$100,000
per year). Treat a posting whose TOP of range sits materially below that as a
seniority downgrade — flag it clearly in "Why It Matches" rather than adding it
silently, and skip it if the title is also below Planner grade.
Always capture the listed salary/range in the spreadsheet when the posting shows
one, so Abdullah can sort/filter himself.

## Employment type
Full-time and Contract roles. Skip part-time and pure internships.

## Location / work arrangement
Any location, any arrangement (remote, hybrid, onsite) — cast a wide net.
Default assumption: prioritize Canada-based or remote-from-Canada-eligible
roles first (Abdullah is Toronto-based, currently works for the City of
Toronto), since work-authorization requirements aren't confirmed for
non-Canada roles. Clearly flag any international role that looks like it
requires sponsorship/relocation so Abdullah can decide.

## Priority employers — enumerate every opening, every run
For these employers, do not rely on keyword search. Go to their careers site and
list ALL current openings, then judge each on the work described:
- Metrolinx (and GO Transit / UP Express)
- City of Toronto
- TTC
- Region of Peel, York Region, Region of Durham, City of Mississauga, City of Brampton
- Major consultancies: WSP, Arcadis, HDR, Stantec, AECOM, Dillon, CIMA+, IBI/Arcadis, Parsons

If a priority employer's site cannot be read on a given run (JavaScript portal,
blocked, error), SAY SO EXPLICITLY in the daily summary. A source that returns
nothing is a finding, not a silence. Never let an unreadable source look like an
employer with no openings.

## Sources to check (in order of priority)
1. Priority-employer career pages (see above), enumerated in full.
2. General web search across company career pages and job boards (Indeed,
   Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, government job boards, engineering-firm
   career pages, transit agency career pages).
3. LinkedIn Jobs — public job search/listing pages only. Never log into
   Abdullah's LinkedIn account or scrape behind a login wall.

## Volume
Aim for the strongest ~10-15 new matches per day. Quality over quantity —
skip anything only loosely related just to hit a number. A light day of
senior-grade matches beats a full day padded with junior roles. A live,
verifiable posting only — never fabricate one, and skip anything that looks
expired or unverifiable.

## De-duplication
Before adding a job, check `seen_jobs.txt` in this same Drive folder. Never
add a job whose URL is already listed there. After adding a job to the
tracker, append its URL to `seen_jobs.txt`.

## Exclusions
- Any title in the "DO NOT include" list under Seniority above.
- Pure civil/structural/construction-management roles with no transportation,
  traffic, GIS, or transit-planning component.
- Roles that explicitly require an active P.Eng license as a hard
  requirement (not "eligible to obtain").
- Sales, marketing, or non-technical roles at transportation companies.
