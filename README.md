## The gaps that keep blocking you

Carried forward from the earlier set, because they have not changed:

1. **No management experience.** It blocked eight of the thirty-three postings. If you have
   ever mentored, supervised a student or co-op, led a working group or directed a
   consultant's scope, tell me — that is the difference between "none" and "early." Otherwise
   asking your City manager for a team-lead or acting assignment is the highest-value move
   available to you. *(117317 does not ask for it.)*
2. **Procurement and contract management.** Counted against eight postings. Getting onto a bid
   package or contract file would open a tier of City roles.
3. **PEO registration.** It was qualification 1 on two postings and gated the Engineer stream
   of a third. **An EIT registration is quick and cheap** and at least puts "EIT, PEO" on the
   page. Separately, **PMP** appears across City PTM2/PTM3 postings and you likely already
   meet the experience hours.
4. **A Class G driver's licence** — required or implied on seven postings.
5. **Power BI**, for BI and performance-reporting roles. Two days of practice would close it.
6. **CROR certification**, which gates rail operations roles. Worth finding out whether an
   employer would sponsor the course. *(Not required for 117317.)*

**If you do nothing else from these notes, deal with items 3 and 4.** Neither is about
experience or seniority. Both are administrative, both are within your control, and together
they have cost you more opportunities than any skills gap in your profile.

## Typography

| Where | Face |
|---|---|
| Name at the top | **Comfortaa** Bold |
| Section headings | **Comfortaa** Bold |
| Body text, job titles, bullets | **Nunito** (Regular / Bold / Italic) |
| Signature on cover letters | **Style Script** |

Nunito carries the body text because it is highly readable at small sizes and parses cleanly
through applicant tracking systems, which matters — the City screens through SAP
SuccessFactors. Comfortaa gives the headings a distinct look without hurting legibility.
Style Script appears once per letter, as the signature, which is the one place a script face
belongs on a job application.

**Poetsen One is deliberately unused.** It is a heavy display face; on a government
application it reads as loud, and it would parse badly in an ATS if used for anything but a
single line. Say the word if you want it on the name and I will switch it.
