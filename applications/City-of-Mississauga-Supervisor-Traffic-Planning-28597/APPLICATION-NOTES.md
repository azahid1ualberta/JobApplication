## Verdict
The work is a direct match: the team reviews development applications, traffic impact studies,
site plans and access permits from a traffic planning view. That is what you do at the City of
Toronto. The step up is supervising the team. The posting asks for 8 years including 3 in
leadership, and you have about 4.5 with none supervising. P.Eng. is only preferred.
