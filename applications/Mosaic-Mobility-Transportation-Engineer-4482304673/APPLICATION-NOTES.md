## Verdict
Good match on the work, with one credential question. The role is transportation impact studies, intersection analysis in Synchro, traffic forecasting and multimodal assessments, which is your City of Toronto development review plus your Mott MacDonald Synchro work. It is pitched at someone with up to five years, so the seniority is right. The posting asks for P.Eng. registration "or eligible for registration"; you are not registered with PEO as an EIT or P.Eng., so confirm with them whether that is a barrier. It is a small firm, so the pay is likely below the City's. The old Metrolinx team is not involved, so the Crew Variance Tool and command line workflow are included.
