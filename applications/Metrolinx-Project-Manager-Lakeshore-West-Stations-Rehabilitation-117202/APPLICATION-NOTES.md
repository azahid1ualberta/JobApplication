(Project Manager, Stations)** in August, which I rated weak for the same reasons. The job is
taking station rehabilitation projects from planning through tender, construction and
commissioning, with the budget, procurement and contracts that come with it.

## Where you do fit

- *"Facilitate subject matter expert review and evaluation of consultant studies, reports,
  design proposals, specifications ... for compliance with Metrolinx policies, procedures and
  standards"* — reviewing engineering submissions, consultant studies and drawings against
  standards is your daily work at the City.
- *"Liaises with ... government agencies, municipal authorities ... and Railways"* — you are on
  the municipal side now and have worked inside the railway.
- **Construction on a live railway.** Your slow-order work at Metrolinx is the operating side of
  exactly this: what station construction does to GO service. The **command line workflow** and
  the **Crew Variance Tool** are both on the resume and in the letter's third paragraph —
  Capital Projects Group is not your old team.

The letter's fourth paragraph names every gap in the table above except the G licence, which
is only an asset.
