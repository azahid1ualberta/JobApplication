## Verdict: right on substance, short on level

The same shape as **116241** (Senior Manager, Network Planning & Strategic Timetabling) earlier:
the analytical core of the job is genuinely yours, but it is a manager's role with staff, and
the qualifications say so. Worth applying as a door-knock.

### Where the substance lines up

> *"Provides teams such as operational planning, **service design**, engineering and safety with
> data analysis inputs into **service planning simulation tools** to assess the impacts to
> new/revised train service plans and future state infrastructure requirements."*

That is your 2023 job seen from the other side. You were in Service Design, running those
simulation tools. The letter opens on this.

> *"Provides **single source data** to senior leaders..."*

The Transportation Data Dashboard, almost word for word: several years of data from separate
operational sources reconciled into one validated source.

> *"Lead analysis, modeling, reporting and insights ... on the safety, risk, and **reliability**
> of our GO Rail ... business."*

Run-time variance across subdivisions is reliability analysis at timetable level. The **Crew
Variance Tool** and the **command line workflow** are both on the resume and named in the
letter's second paragraph. This is Engineering & Safety, not your old team, so there is no
reason to hold them back.

### What is missing

| Requirement | Where you stand |
|---|---|
| *"Demonstrated years of leadership experience"*; leads a team of senior and junior advisors | You have not managed staff. The main blocker, as on eight earlier postings. |
| **RAMS regime per EN 50126** | Not done. This is a named responsibility, not a nice-to-have. |
| Liquidated damages and vendor accountability | Not done. |
| Lean, root cause, Plan-Do-Check-Act | Not formally. |
| Expense allocation, cost management | Not done. |
| Monthly and annual forecasting of safety, risk and reliability performance | Not done as such. |
| P.Eng. | Not held — asset only. |

The letter's fourth paragraph names all of these, then asks to be considered for an
**advisor-level** position in the same office. The posting mentions a team of *"technical
senior and junior performance advisors"*, so those roles exist; that is the realistic target.

## Before you apply

- Read a short primer on **EN 50126 and RAMS** (Reliability, Availability, Maintainability,
  Safety). You will not be expected to know it deeply for an advisor role, but knowing what
  it covers shows you understand the job.
- Look out for a **Senior Performance Advisor** or similar posting from the same office. If
  one appears, it is likely a closer fit than this manager role.
